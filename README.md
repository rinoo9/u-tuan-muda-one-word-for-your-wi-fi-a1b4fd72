# One Word for Your Wi-Fi — "Peekaboo"

A one-word entry for the make.fun bounty "One Word for Your Wi-Fi". The program `main.py` writes the single word "Peekaboo" to `/output/entry.txt`, with nothing else: no newline, no extra words.

Why "Peekaboo"? Home Wi-Fi is there one second and gone the next — full bars in the living room, nothing in the bedroom, back again when you stand in the doorway. It doesn't just go slow; it plays peekaboo with you. The joke needs no explanation, and as a network name it is one people would smile at and remember.

Run it with `python3 main.py` (Python 3.13).
