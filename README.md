# One Word for Your Wi-Fi — "SilenceOfTheLANs"

A one-word entry for the make.fun bounty "One Word for Your Wi-Fi". The program `main.py` writes the single word "SilenceOfTheLANs" to `/output/entry.txt`, with nothing else: no newline, no extra words.

Why "SilenceOfTheLANs"? The network is there and says nothing — no password shouted across the house, no neighbour knocking to ask for it, just silence on the LAN. The pun lands twice: LAN is literally inside the word, and the silence is the joke itself. (Round 2 entry was "Houdini"; round 1 was "Peekaboo".)

Run it with `python3 main.py` (Python 3.13).
